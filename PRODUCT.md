# APORIA

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: Vite, React, Three.js and a project-local Node server with SQLite. All inference uses the installed local Ollama service.

## Users
Philosophy students and researchers developing paper directions from published arguments, alongside the project owner conducting philosophical cognition experiments on their Mac.

## Product Purpose
Develop provisional philosophical paper directions by retrieving literature, reconstructing published arguments, testing objections and assessing revised proposals. The same local laboratory investigates whether explicit cognitive functions and differentiated computational policies produce different philosophical trajectories from the same question.

## Operating Context
A localhost laboratory, launched with one command after local dependencies are installed. A central continuously deforming particle object introduces the experience; research results are available below it. Paper-based discovery is the default research workflow; cognitive experiments remain selectable in Experiment setup. Discovery records expose the existing cognitive laboratory through a native disclosure. Finished cognitive records can feed their objections into a new paper-based discovery run for the same question.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Paper-based discovery retrieves OpenAlex literature and available open-access source text, selects arguments, reconstructs quoted premises and conclusions, indexes claims and abstracts, connects the five cognitive profiles to objections, checks objection novelty, runs debates, produces research briefs, assesses academic quality, revises proposals, reassesses revisions and checks final contributions against retrieved literature. The interface provides Directions, Papers, Debates and Research log views, pause/resume/stop controls, Markdown brief downloads and JSON research export. Source availability, source digests, verified and struck citation IDs, model assignments and failures remain inspectable.

Research directions are ranked only when academic quality and final contribution novelty have been assessed. A successfully assessed revision supersedes the original for display and ranking; the original proposal and assessment remain inspectable. Missing scores say “Not assessed”; completed briefs excluded from ranking say “Unranked” and explain why. Novelty concerns the retrieved corpus, not established originality. Academic grades and debate outcomes are model judgments, not independent human evaluation or journal acceptance. Separate roles from one installed model family do not establish independent family comparisons.

The cognitive engine retains fresh-memory comparisons and explicit prior-memory learning, exportable provenance, current-hypothesis evidence coverage, resumable local studies, typed argument graph, state-based controller, curiosity, metacognition, counterfactual tests, policy learning, five cognitive profiles, measured diversity and experiment export. Δ changes actual policies. No forced disagreement or fictional model outputs. Manual visualization modes must be labeled simulation. Keep inference, dependencies and research records project-local; literature retrieval requires external source access. No deployment, destructive actions, secrets, or system configuration changes.

## Brand Commitments
Restrained dark scientific instrument; spatial folded particle topology; blue/cyan/violet with warm conflict regions. No robot avatars, chat interface, neon HUD, generic sphere, or full platform.

## Evidence on Hand
The workspace began empty. LobBot source is available in the sibling LOBBOT/github-publish folder. Local Ollama models exist. The Crux Lab paper-discovery loop is integrated with the stateful cognitive engine. Desktop review captures use a recorded upstream fixture through browser interception; they establish interface behavior and presentation, not the quality of a newly completed local discovery run. Compression does not yet establish a scientific cognitive-diversity result.

## Product Principles
- Observable state controls routing and visualization.
- All profiles receive the same question; disagreement is measured, never rewarded.
- Record assumptions, provenance and failed experiments.
- Keep the visual object foremost and inspection tools secondary.
- Preserve the original and revised proposals, source boundaries and missing assessments so provisional paper directions can be critically reviewed.

## Open Decisions
APORIA and the original Divergent Apertures symbol are the approved identity. Scientific validity of diversity metrics and model compression remains experimental. Source-based model assessment and literature-relative novelty checks are implemented; independent human evaluation remains unimplemented. Their academic validity and the scholarly value of generated paper directions still require independent evaluation.
