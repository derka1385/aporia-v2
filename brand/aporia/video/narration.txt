For philosophy students, the hardest question is often what to write next.

A convincing AI answer can miss a hidden premise, or repeat an old objection.

Meet Aporia. An experimental AI lab for philosophical research.

It reads the literature, rebuilds arguments from source quotes, and brings hidden assumptions into view.

Five cognitive profiles investigate the same question. Delta changes their actual reasoning policies, opening different paths.

Objections face a prior-art check and two independent defenders. A referee labels the outcome and verifies citations.

The Director learns what to test next. Surviving leads become ranked briefs, with the strongest replies and what your paper must show.

Aporia. Your next research direction. The final judgment stays yours.
